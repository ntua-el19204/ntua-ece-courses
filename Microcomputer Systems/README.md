# Microcomputer Systems 

Solutions in Assembly 8085 to the programming labs developed by the **Microprocessors Laboratory & Digital Systems Laboratory** of the **School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, National Technical University of Athens (NTUA)**,
as part of the *Microcomputer Systems* course during the **Spring 2021–2022**.
